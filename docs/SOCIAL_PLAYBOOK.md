# Social sharing playbook

## Canonical URLs

Always share **non-www** links:

`https://michigandance.life/event/{slug}`

## UTM parameters (optional)

Append for attribution in analytics:

`?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=event-{slug}`

## What to post

- New approved events (image + date + venue + link)
- Weekly “this week in Michigan dance” → `/events`
- City-specific posts → `/events/city/{city-slug}`

## Platforms

Instagram/Facebook: link in bio to homepage; stories with event deep links.
